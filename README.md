# Insurance Game Theory

**Game Theory Analysis: How the Thompson Shooting Changed Health Insurance**

A data-driven analysis of how the December 2024 killing of UnitedHealthcare CEO Brian Thompson revealed a catastrophic failure in the game theory of health insurance denial practices, and why the industry's old equilibrium can never return.

## Live Site

[https://rayhe.github.io/insurance-game-theory](https://rayhe.github.io/insurance-game-theory)

## What This Covers

1. **The Facts** — Pre-shooting denial rates, AI algorithms, stock price collapse, CEO resignation, shareholder lawsuits, industry reform pledges
2. **The Game Theory** — Asymmetric risk tolerance, repeated vs. one-shot games, credible threat establishment, mechanism design failure, information asymmetry collapse, prisoner's dilemma of reform
3. **The New Equilibrium** — Three possible stable states (genuine reform, security fortress, regulatory capitulation) and their relative costs
4. **Implications** — What this means for any industry that creates populations with nothing to lose

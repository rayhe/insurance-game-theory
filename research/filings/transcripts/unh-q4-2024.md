# UNH Q4 2024 Earnings Call — January 16, 2025
## First Earnings Call After Brian Thompson Murder (Dec 4, 2024)

**Source:** Insider Monkey (full transcript)

### Key Facts
- Adjusted EPS: $6.81 (beat $6.74 estimate)
- Revenue: $100.8B (missed $101.7B estimate)
- FY2024 MCR: 85.5% (up 200bps YoY)
- FY2024 adjusted EPS: $27.66
- FY2025 guidance: $29.50-$30.00 EPS, $450-$455B revenue

### Thompson Tribute
Call opened with tribute. Witty: "sincere thank you... following the murder of our friend, Brian Thompson."
Rex: "Brian helped build this company and forged deep trusted relationships for over 20 years."

### PA Reform Signal
Witty: "We're eager to work with policy leaders to use standardization and technology to speed up turnaround times for approval of procedures and services for Medicare Advantage patients and to materially reduce the overall number of prior authorizations used for certain MA services."

### System Reform Rhetoric
Witty: "Healthcare costs more in the U.S., because the price of a single procedure, visit or prescription is higher here than it is in other countries."
On PBMs: "Optum Rx alone delivers many tens of billions of dollars in savings annually versus the pricing set by the manufacturers"
100% rebate pass-through commitment by 2028.

### Key Metrics
- Medicare MCR: elevated, 150bps above original FY2024 outlook
- $6B in unanticipated impacts (rate cuts + cyberattack)
- FY2025 MCR guidance: 86.5% ± 50bps

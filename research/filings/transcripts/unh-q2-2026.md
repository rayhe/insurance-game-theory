# UNH Q2 2026 Earnings Call — July 16, 2026
## Recovery Call — Beat Expectations, PA Reform Commitments

**Source:** MarketBeat (full transcript, 1443 lines)

### Key Facts
- Beat expectations on adjusted earnings
- Medicare cost trend coming in below initial 10% estimate
- MA membership decline revised to ~1.1M (better than 1.3-1.4M expected)
- Medicare margins expected above 3% for FY2026
- Commercial costs stubbornly high (>11%)

### Hemsley Opening
"Our second quarter results and updated full year 2026 outlook demonstrate continuing progress toward delivering more consistent and dependable performance."
"AI technology is helping us move faster. We're using it to improve service interactions, reduce administrative burden, and support better decision-making."

### PA Reform — Tim Noel
"In the quarter, we committed to eliminating, by the end of this year, 30% of prior authorization volume and nearly two-thirds of prior authorization requirements for pediatric care."
"AI is both an enabler and accelerant to this effort."
Target: 80% of PAs processed in real time by end of 2027.

### Optum Progress — Patrick Conway
"Enhanced support for patients during key transitions of care has resulted in approximately 10% reduction in hospitalizations"
"Home health initiatives... driven a more than 20% improvement in timely care delivery"
"AI-based ambient listening capabilities, available to 70% of our employed providers today and on track to exceed 90% by year-end"
"Patient experience in our care delivery sites is up approximately 5% year-over-year"
"Patient access has expanded by nearly 200,000 more patient-facing hours"

### Financial Outlook
- Medicare trend below initial 10% estimate
- Commercial trend "modestly above 11%"
- Commercial margin recovery "will remain a focus area longer than originally anticipated"
- Management "very much feel that we can operate within our long-term growth rate margins, that 13% to 16%"

# UNH Q1 2025 Earnings Call — April 17, 2025
## The "Crash Call" — Guidance Cut, Stock -20%+ in One Day

**Source:** StockAnalysis.com (full transcript, 764 lines)

### Key Facts
- Guidance slashed: $29.50-$30.00 → $26.00-$26.50
- Medicare care activity 2x expected rate
- Stock dropped >20% in a single session

### Andrew Witty's Opening
"UnitedHealth Group started 2025 in two seemingly disparate ways. One: continued strong growth across our businesses... The other way, however, was an overall performance that was frankly unusual and unacceptable."

### Medicare Utilization Shock
Tim Noel: "What we are seeing... driving an overall two-times increase in that level of units consumed in Q1 of 2025."
"A dynamic at play in our group Medicare Advantage business is we're seeing a significant and disproportionate increase in utilization, largely within our public sector group retiree business."
"Kind of think groups with premiums going from $50 to $200."

### PA Reform Announcement

### AI Deployment

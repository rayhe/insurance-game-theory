## Prior Authorization Reform

### Q1 2025 Call (Apr 17, 2025) — Andrew Witty
> "Optum Rx will remove prior authorizations on 80 drugs accounting for more than 10% of our pharmaceutical prior authorizations."

### Q2 2026 Call (Jul 16, 2026) — Tim Noel (CEO, UnitedHealthcare)
> "In the quarter, we committed to eliminating, by the end of this year, 30% of prior authorization volume and nearly two-thirds of prior authorization requirements for pediatric care."

> "We continue to take concrete steps to reduce complexity and increase speed by further simplifying prior authorization, increasing consumer responsive digital experiences, providing greater support to rural hospitals and care providers."

### Zacks Analysis (Jul 17, 2026):
> "UnitedHealth said it plans to eliminate 30% of prior authorization volume by the end of 2026, remove nearly two-thirds of pediatric prior authorization requirements and publicly report prior authorization metrics."

> "Noel said the company aims to process 80% of prior authorizations in real time by the end of 2027."

### AHIP / Industry Pledge (Jun 23, 2025):
- ~50 insurers signed voluntary pledge including all Big 6: UnitedHealthcare, Elevance, Centene, Cigna, CVS/Aetna, Humana
- 6 key commitments: reduce PA volume, standardize electronic submissions (FHIR), real-time decisions for 80% by 2027, honor approvals across plan changes, transparency, medical professional review of all clinical denials
- Covers plans insuring ~80% / 257 million Americans

### AHIP Progress Report (Apr 2026):
> "Leading health plans reduced prior authorizations for an array of services by 11% since the pledge was made. This equates to 6.5 million fewer prior auth requests for patients."
> "Reductions in Medicare Advantage specifically were 15%."

## AI in Coverage Decisions

### Q1 2025 Call — Andrew Witty
> "26 million consumer calls were more accurately directed to the right advocate by an AI agent, improving the consumer experience and reducing wait times. We expect AI will direct over half of our calls to the best resource during 2025."

### Q4 2025 Call (Jan 27, 2026) — Stephen Hemsley
> "We are driving greater operational disciplines in all our business practices, leveraging the use of technology and artificial intelligence broadly, and renewing our commitment to innovation, agility, and accountability."

> "We plan to be a leader in that [AI] movement."

### Q2 2026 Call — Stephen Hemsley
> "AI technology is helping us move faster. We're using it to improve service interactions, reduce administrative burden, and support better decision-making, always in service of improved experiences and outcomes for both patients and care providers."

### Q2 2026 Call — Patrick Conway (CEO, Optum)
> "There is real progress on the rollout of AI-based ambient listening capabilities, available to 70% of our employed providers today and on track to exceed 90% by year-end."

### DEF 14A FY2025:
- Performance shares now tied to "cyber security and artificial intelligence" metrics
- ~$1 billion in targeted cost savings for 2026 "primarily from AI and machine learning initiatives"
- Planned $1.5B tech/AI investment in 2026, similar in 2027
- 2,000+ engineers deployed on AI; 1,000+ use cases

## MCR Changes / Medical Cost Trends

### Q4 2024 Call (Jan 16, 2025) — John Rex
> "We expect a '25 full year medical care ratio of 86.5%, plus or minus 50 basis points, 100 basis points above the '24 result."
> "The first [element] comprise about 70% of the total impact... the mix of people served."

### Q1 2025 Call (Apr 17, 2025) — Andrew Witty
> "Our performance... was frankly unusual and unacceptable."
> "First quarter 2025 indications suggest care activity increased at twice that rate [vs 2024]."

### Q1 2025 Call — John Rex
> "We now expect adjusted earnings of $26-$26.50 per share. It's an outlook that I'm extremely disappointed to share with you."
> MCR revised to 87.5% ± 50 bps

### Q4 2025 Call (Jan 27, 2026) — Timothy Noel
> "The 2025 medical cost trend was in line with our expectation of approximately 7.5% and supports our 2026 trend expectation of 10%."

### Q2 2026 Call — Tim Noel
> "Medical cost trends in Medicare are still running well above historical levels, but below our expectations so far in 2026."
> "Commercial costs are stubbornly high, rising above expectations."
> "We expect the 2026 Medicare medical cost trend to come in below our initial estimate of around 10%."
> "Commercial... medical cost trends modestly above 11% level we previously saw."

## Regulatory Reform / Industry Response

### Q4 2024 Call — Andrew Witty
> "We're eager to work with policy leaders to use standardization and technology to speed up turnaround times for approval of procedures and services for Medicare Advantage patients and to materially reduce the overall number of prior authorizations used for certain MA services."

## Stock Price / Financial Impact

### Becker's Retrospective (Dec 2025):
> "UnitedHealth Group... watched its stock plunge by around 50%"
> "UnitedHealth now expects $6.5 billion more in medical costs for 2025 than originally anticipated"
> "The company posted a $2.3 billion profit in the third quarter of 2025, down from a $6.1 billion profit during the same time last year"

### Q4 2025 Call:
- FY2025 adjusted EPS: $16.35 (vs original guidance of $29.50-$30.00 → revised to $26-$26.50)
- Revenue: ~$448B (+12% YoY)
- MCR: 89.1% (vs original guidance 86.5%)
- $1.6B net-of-tax non-cash charge in Q4

### Q2 2026 Call:
- Medicare margins expected to finish 2026 above 3%
- MA membership decline now ~1.1M (better than 1.3-1.4M expected)
- Medicare cost trend coming in below initial 10% estimate
- EPS guidance raised

### FY2022 (Pre-Shooting)
**NO "Executive Security" column — no security spending disclosed**
Same pattern: only 401(k), insurance, relocation, tax equalization disclosed.

### Key Finding
Executive security was a **zero-disclosure item** pre-shooting. The Dec 4, 2024 murder of Brian Thompson created a regime change:
- FY2024 proxy (filed Apr 2025): First ever disclosure of executive security as a line item (~$1.7M total)
- FY2025 proxy (filed Apr 2026): Continued and expanded (~$1.7M total, now with new executives)
- Heather Cianfrocco's $926,989 in FY2024 was the single largest individual security expenditure
- Timothy Noel (Thompson's successor) received the highest security in FY2025 ($448,276)

## Prior Authorization & AI Reforms Referenced in Proxies

### FY2025 Proxy (2026)
- "We have advanced a growing reform agenda across Optum and UnitedHealthcare in areas such as data and process interoperability, prior authorization, transparency, pharmacy practices"
- "This year we have begun to publish results in additional key areas such as prior authorizations and claim approval rates, performance statistics, data and trends, rebate practices"
- Performance shares tied to "enterprise and business segment financial results, cost reduction, **cyber security and artificial intelligence**"
- New **Health and Clinical Practice Policies Committee** on the Board — oversees "quality and patient safety"
- AI referenced in long-term incentive performance criteria

### FY2024 Proxy (2025)
- No PA reform mentions (pre-shooting filing, but covers period including Dec 2024 shooting)
- Shareholder proposal requesting "Shareholder Vote Regarding Excessive Golden Parachutes" — Board recommended AGAINST
- Cybersecurity oversight expanded post-Change Healthcare breach

### FY2023 Proxy (2024)
- Change Healthcare cyberattack referenced: "Audit and Finance Committee will continue to review this incident"
- No PA reform or denial rate disclosures

### FY2022 Proxy (2023)
- Standard cybersecurity and data protection language
- No PA, denial, or reform mentions

  - Context: Hemsley holds both Chair and CEO roles, breaking longstanding UNH practice
  - Board: "departed from its long-standing practice of having separate individuals as CEO and Chair given the extraordinary circumstances"

### FY2024 (2025 Proxy)
